# SILLAGE Intelligence · Art direction 01

Status: Golden Screen candidate; human visual approval pending. Scope: `/live` only.

## Product and hierarchy

The V2 development plan governs business semantics. The supplied Luxury UI/UX prompt governs this implementation. The breakdown prompt is intentionally not executed. No additional business pages or downstream task cards are included.

Camera first, interpretation second, navigation third. The first view must communicate an entrance camera translating movement into explainable counts. A calm ivory architectural frame surrounds a charcoal camera instrument. The warm synthetic interior supplies material character; gold is never a background theme.

## Executable direction

- Header 72px, compact navigation 76px; navigation can fully disappear. Expanded navigation 224px.
- Content padding 32px at 1440px, 40px above 1700px, 24px below 1400px.
- Main area: minmax(0, 1fr) + 280px event rail, 24px gap. Image and direct controls receive approximately 70–75% of the default workspace width. Focus mode removes the shell, rail, and camera list.
- Camera aperture 16:9. Image is the largest continuous surface, without a decorative enclosing card. The playhead and count strip are contiguous with it.
- Brand and page headings: locally bundled Cormorant Garamond, regular/medium. Interface: locally bundled DM Sans 400/500/600. Tabular numerals throughout telemetry. No remote font requests.
- Ink #232521, ivory #F5F2EB, camera surface #171916, brass #B9A27D. Sage denotes entries and device readiness; clay denotes exits. Semantic data colors are consistent in chart, event rail, and overlays.
- Page title 44px, brand 25px, key metrics 30px, main controls 12–13px, metadata 10–11px. Uppercase tracking only for small section labels.
- Rectangular architecture with purposeful radii: 2px tags, 4px controls, 8px floating panels. The camera has a sharp aperture; no glowing outlines or glass cards.
- Thin separators organize groups; containers do not each receive a border. Warm paper is reserved for the event rail and interpretation surfaces.
- Motion: 160ms interaction transitions; the synthetic scene is driven exclusively by replay time. Reduced motion removes decorative transitions; playback remains explicitly user controlled.

## Camera art

An original code-generated store interior: limestone floor, dark walnut shelves, warm plaster, a sculptural consultation counter, small bottle arrays, and anonymous mannequin-like people. A fixed elevated camera observes the entrance threshold. This is functional 3D scene geometry, not a marketing image or decorative floating object. A permanent `SIMULATED CAMERA SCENE` label prevents confusion with real footage.

People, projected bounding boxes, trajectory history, crossing line, timestamps, event rail, and metrics share normalized replay time. Confidence values are explicitly fixture metadata, not measured model accuracy.

## Interaction direction

Primary action is play/pause. Overlay and trajectory switches are secondary; camera and scenario selection are contextual. The event rail supports All / Entries / Exits filters and opens event details. A drawer explains visit/track/identity boundaries. Unbuilt destinations are labeled Next phase and open a scope notice rather than pretend to be complete pages.

There is no automatic design freeze. Browser evidence and a revision record accompany this candidate; final visual acceptance belongs to the user.
